Privacy Policy

A straightforward account of this prototype.

Publication and admin data

The server-backed version stores posts, administrator email addresses, password hashes, and admin session records in the configured MongoDB database. Passwords are hashed before storage. Uploaded images are stored in the server’s configured upload directory. Published posts and uploaded images are publicly accessible. Uploaded file URLs are public even when attached to drafts; draft post records require an authenticated admin session.

Admin session cookie

Signing in creates an HTTP-only session cookie used to authenticate admin requests. The session has a maximum lifetime of eight hours and is revoked on sign-out. This cookie is for administration, not advertising or analytics. No admin credentials or session tokens are stored in browser local storage.

Forms on the public site

The newsletter and contact forms remain demonstrations. Their supplied implementations validate entries in the browser without sending or storing them. No advertising or analytics scripts are included.

Fonts, hosting, and database provider

The Source Sans 3 fonts and site images are served from this website. Reading pages do not request fonts from an external font provider. The site operator’s hosting and MongoDB Atlas configuration determine infrastructure logging, database region, backups, and retention. The operator must provide its verified identity, contact details, and applicable retention information before production launch.

Sharing and external links

Source links lead to third-party websites with their own privacy practices. Copy-link tools write the post URL to your clipboard when requested. Native sharing uses your device’s share interface.

Removal and retention

Removing a post deletes its database record. Image files are retained because they may be referenced by other posts and require separate storage maintenance. Backup retention depends on the operator’s configured providers.

Contact

A verified privacy contact has not yet been configured. The public contact form does not deliver messages. Operator details and a working contact channel are required before production launch.
