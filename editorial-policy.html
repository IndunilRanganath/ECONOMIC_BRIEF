OUR STANDARDS

Editorial Policy

Clear thinking begins with accountable publishing.

These are the proposed standards for this prototype, not a claim of an established newsroom process.

Accuracy and verification

Factual claims must be checked against reliable original sources before publication. Current affiliations, statistics, forecasts, and policy decisions require a dated verification. We do not invent quotations, financial figures, credentials, or breaking news.

Sourcing and context

We favour original research, official statistical releases, and primary documentation. Source links should allow readers to inspect the evidence. Estimates and forecasts must be attributed and clearly distinguished from outcomes.

Analysis and opinion

Analysis explains evidence and uncertainty. Opinion offers a viewpoint and must be labelled. Neither should be presented as a guaranteed prediction. Relevant conflicts and commercial relationships must be disclosed.

Corrections

Readers should be able to identify the article, the disputed passage, and supporting evidence when requesting a correction. Substantive corrections should carry an explanation and an accurate update date. The prototype’s contact form does not yet deliver messages; a verified correction channel is required before launch.

Financial education

Our educational material is general. It does not consider a reader’s individual circumstances and does not promise returns or constitute personalized investment advice.

Demonstration content

Sample articles on this site are labelled demonstrations. No genuine publication or update dates are assigned to drafts. The article about economic experts preserves the owner’s complete supplied content; time-sensitive claims must be reviewed before publication.
